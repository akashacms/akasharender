# 2026-08-14 13:42 - Add "record URL vs. self-host" comparison and no-conversion note to favicon answer

Expanded the favicon answer page in response to two follow-up points from the
user: (1) browsers display ICO natively, so no conversion to JPG is needed; and
(2) recording the discovered favicon URL (without downloading it) is a simpler
mechanism that still bypasses Google.

## Changes

- Reworked the "Recommended direct-discovery design" section so that discovery
  and hostname caching are presented as shared build-time steps, independent of
  how the icon is finally emitted.
- Added a new section **"Two emit strategies: record the URL vs. self-host"**:
  - Strategy A (self-host / download): most private (no third party sees the
    visitor) and most stable (icon captured at build time), at the cost of extra
    build work, bytes, and format handling.
  - Strategy B (record the discovered URL): simpler, removes the Google request,
    but only a *partial* privacy improvement because the visitor's browser still
    makes a cross-origin request to each linked site (shifting tracking from
    Google to the target sites), and is less stable (hotlink breakage,
    mixed-content/CORS footguns).
  - Recommends making this a config choice (`faviconMode`), defaulting to
    self-host.
- Added a section clarifying the deferred `<i class='ak-favicon-ref'>` rewrite
  is **orthogonal to privacy** (the browser still fetches from the named origin;
  its benefit is avoiding stray `<img>` tags that confuse Medium imports).
- Added a section **"No conversion needed — serve ICO/PNG/SVG as-is"**: browsers
  decode ICO natively, converting to JPG is harmful (no transparency, lossy,
  discards multi-size ICO container); any re-encoding should be for
  resizing/normalization only and stay in PNG/SVG.
- Updated the "Practical caveats" bullets to scope resizing/format-normalization
  to the self-host strategy and reiterate no display-time conversion.
- Updated the page `last-updated` frontmatter.
- Updated the summaries in `wiki/answers/README.md` and `wiki/index.md` to
  mention the two emit strategies and the no-JPG-conversion point, and bumped the
  `wiki/index.md` `last-updated` timestamp.
